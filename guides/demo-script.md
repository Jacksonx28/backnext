# BackNext: 60–90 second demo

**0–12 sec — Problem**
“Creators have more ideas than time. Fans can say what they want, but that interest rarely connects clearly to support and finished work. BackNext puts that loop in one place.”

**12–30 sec — Back a useful result**
Show the six requests. Open “Turn one video into a week of content.” Point out its concrete takeaway. Add 100 simulated WIF. Show the confirmation and demo receipt.
“Each request describes a specific result. Fans back the work they want to see. This version uses simulated backing—no wallet or real money.”

**30–50 sec — Create**
Open Creator studio. Add a request you can personally deliver, with a clear title, description, and goal.
“Creators publish a small menu of useful work instead of asking for support without a specific outcome.”

**50–70 sec — Deliver**
Use Add result on a request and attach a genuine link to finished work. Switch to Delivered and open it.
“The finished result stays attached to the request. That closes the loop and gives fans a reason to return for the next idea.”

**70–90 sec — Honest status**
“BackNext is a working browser-local prototype. Wallet integration and onchain payments are future work. Our next validation is testing this flow with creators and fans before introducing real funds.”

Record in a fresh browser profile, or use Reset demo before recording. Do not present demo receipts, simulated totals, or sample requests as real traction. Get permission before showing another person's private information or work.
